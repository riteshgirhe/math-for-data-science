# Mathematics for Data Science

This repository documents my structured preparation in the mathematics, statistics, and ML foundations required for Data Science interviews — organized into 12 phases, 57 topics.

Every topic here follows the same rule: **no topic is marked done until there's a from-scratch implementation or a solved problem set to prove it** — not just notes. Notes alone don't survive an interview; working code and worked examples do.

🔗 Following along on [LinkedIn](#) · Full tracker on [Notion](#)

---

## 🧭 How This Repo Works

Each notebook in a phase folder contains:
1. A short explanation of the concept, in my own words
2. A from-scratch implementation (no library shortcuts, where applicable)
3. A worked example or a comparison against the library version (numpy/scipy/sklearn), to confirm correctness

If you only want the proof, not the theory, jump straight to the code — every notebook is self-contained.

## 📚 Learning Roadmap

**Phase 1 — Math Foundations**
Number systems · Algebra · Linear & quadratic equations · Functions · Exponents & logarithms · Sequences & series · Complex numbers · Feature scaling & activation functions

**Phase 2 — Logic, Sets & Counting**
Sets & relations · Propositional logic · Permutations & combinations · Counting principles

**Phase 3 — Descriptive Statistics**
Mean, median, mode · Variance, standard deviation, IQR, MAD · Percentiles & quartiles · Skewness & kurtosis · EDA

**Phase 4 — Probability**
Sample spaces & axioms · Conditional probability & independence · Bayes' theorem · Sampling methods

**Phase 5 — Calculus**
Limits & continuity · Chain/product/quotient rules · Partial derivatives & gradients · Taylor series

**Phase 6 — Random Variables & Distributions**
Discrete & continuous RVs · Expectation & variance · Bernoulli, binomial, Poisson · Normal & exponential · Joint/marginal/conditional distributions

**Phase 7 — Linear Algebra**
Vectors & matrices · Determinants & rank · Eigenvalues & eigenvectors · SVD · PCA & dimensionality reduction

**Phase 8 — Sampling, Estimation & Hypothesis Testing**
Sampling bias & variance · MLE · Confidence intervals · p-values & significance · Type I/II errors · z/t/chi-square tests, ANOVA · A/B testing · Correlation

**Phase 9 — Regression & ML Foundations**
Linear & logistic regression · Gradient descent · Ridge & Lasso · Backpropagation

**Phase 10 — Bayesian Statistics**
Priors & posteriors · Gibbs sampling & Metropolis-Hastings · Naive Bayes classifiers

**Phase 11 — Time Series**
Stationarity & differencing · ACF/PACF · ARIMA & SARIMA

**Phase 12 — Optimization**
Lagrange multipliers · Simplex algorithm · Simulated annealing & genetic algorithms
